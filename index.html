Stonks, a stock ticker and watchlist for Omarchy

Today's move in your bar, and a chart you can scrub a click away.

Stonks is a free stock ticker and watchlist for the Omarchy 4 bar. It follows stocks, ETFs, indexes, and crypto, in your theme's colors, with no account and no API key.

INSTALL
$ omarchy plugin add https://github.com/gmchande/omarchy-stonks.git --enable

Needs Omarchy 4, which asks you to confirm, then where in the bar to put the pill. Stonks is on the Omarchy plugin marketplace.

STAGE3SOUND TEST

A chart you can hear.

Run the pointer along the chart and it plays like a row of chimes, higher where the line is higher, and each step of ← → rings too. A replay sweeps the chimes along the line. Smooth and retro each have their own voice.

  • A short sound when you add a symbol, remove a row, or undo the removal, which plays the removal backwards, and a shutter when you take a screenshot.
  • A soft bell as the market opens and as it closes, never while every screen is off or an app records from your microphone.
  • Nothing else sounds by itself: no refresh or price.
  • Click the speaker at the top right, or press m, to turn them off. Your system volume sets how loud they are.

STAGE4SCREENSHOTS

A picture of the chart, laid out to share.

Click the camera at the top right, or press Ctrl+C, for a picture of the chart you're looking at.

  • The price large with its currency, its move, the day's bull or bear, the exchange, and the time of the price with its zone.
  • On a longer range, its high and low on the chart. At the foot, the day's date or the range's length.
  • Scrubbed, it shows the moment you scrubbed to. Your list is never in it.
  • It lands where Omarchy's own screenshots do: a file in your screenshots folder, the picture on the clipboard, and a notification that opens it in your screenshot editor.

STAGE5ON THE LABEL

What's in the cartridge.

  • Click the pill for the day's chart and scrub it with the pointer. Charts go from one day to all time, each with its high and low marked.
  • Pre-market, after-hours, and overnight prices, and when the market opens or closes. Until the next session opens, the day's chart shows the last session, then the night and the pre-market.
  • Stocks, ETFs, indexes, and crypto, from New York to London and Tokyo, each in its own currency.
  • The past 52 weeks' low and high, beside the market cap and P/E.
  • A tiled window too: the popup's chart and rows at the window's width.
  • An old price says "As of" and its time, and a refresh that fails puts a "!" on the pill. When Yahoo sends no prices at all, the header says "Can't get prices" once, not on every row.
  • No account and no API key.

NOT IN THE CARTRIDGE

Stonks doesn't track holdings, send price alerts, or convert prices into another currency.

STAGE6YOUR LISTS

Lists of your own.

All holds every symbol you follow. Your own lists, such as a portfolio or a sector, hold some of them. Click the list's name to switch lists, start one, or manage them.

  • Ctrl-click a row to choose the lists it's in.
  • Remove a row with the × beside its symbol, or Delete. Right-click it to choose where it goes from, such as "Remove from all 3 lists". Removing a symbol from All removes it everywhere.
  • Removed one by mistake? For 5 seconds, "Undo NVDA" shows beside "+ Add a symbol": click it to bring the row back. Until a list next changes, Ctrl+Z does the same.
  • Click the sign at the end of the list's header to show every row's move as a percent, an amount, or since the open.
  • Sort by symbol, name, or change, or keep your own order.

STAGE9HOUSE RULES

What Stonks runs, fetches, and writes.

PRICES
Quotes, charts, and search come from Yahoo's unofficial endpoints, and overnight prices from Robinhood. Either may change or stop without notice.
DELAYS
Yahoo delays many exchanges' quotes, London's and Tokyo's among them, by up to 30 minutes. Any price more than two minutes old while its market trades says "As of" and its time.
RUNS
curl for every request. PipeWire's pw-play for each sound, pw-cat playing silence while you scrub, so each chime plays at once, and pw-dump as a bell is due, to see whether an app records from a microphone. For a screenshot, wl-copy to put it on the clipboard, and Omarchy's notification. omarchy restart shell, only when you click the notice that an update is on disk. omarchy plugin update grvc.stonks, in a terminal, only when you click "Update to …" or press Shift+U.
SITES
query1.finance.yahoo.com for quotes, charts, and the market cap and P/E; query2.finance.yahoo.com for search; api.robinhood.com for overnight prices and which stocks trade overnight; and raw.githubusercontent.com for the newest version number, as the shell starts and then once a day. Nothing else.
FILES
Your lists and settings in ~/.config/omarchy/grvc.stonks.json. In ~/.cache/grvc.stonks, the last quotes and, while sounds are on, the sounds it makes from the chart. Each screenshot you take, in the folder Omarchy's own screenshots use.
UPDATES
Never by itself. When a newer version is out, the footer offers it.
ADVICE
None. Stonks isn't investment advice, and isn't affiliated with Yahoo or Robinhood.

STAGE10QUESTIONS

Asked and answered.

What is Stonks?

Stonks is a free stock ticker and watchlist for the Omarchy 4 bar. A small pill shows how a stock is doing today, and a click opens its chart, with your lists under it.

How do I install a stock ticker on Omarchy?

Install Stonks with one command: omarchy plugin add https://github.com/gmchande/omarchy-stonks.git --enable. It needs Omarchy 4, which asks you to confirm, then where in the bar to put the pill.

Does Stonks need an account or an API key?

No. Stonks needs no account and no API key.

Where do Stonks' prices come from?

Stonks gets quotes, charts, and search from Yahoo Finance's unofficial endpoints, and overnight prices from Robinhood. Yahoo delays many exchanges' quotes by up to 30 minutes, London's and Tokyo's among them, and Stonks marks an old price with "As of" and its time.

Does Stonks show pre-market, after-hours, and overnight prices?

Yes. Stonks shows the pre-market, after-hours, or overnight price on a smaller line under the price. Overnight prices are for the US stocks and ETFs that Robinhood trades overnight. Until the next session opens, the day's chart shows the last session, then the night and the pre-market.

Which markets does Stonks follow?

Stonks follows stocks, ETFs, indexes, and crypto, from New York to London and Tokyo, each in its own currency. It never converts a price into another currency.

Does Stonks make sounds?

Yes, short ones: when you add a symbol, remove a row, or undo the removal, and a shutter when you take a screenshot. Moving along the chart plays it like a row of chimes, and a soft bell rings as the market opens and closes. Click the speaker at the top right, or press m, to turn them off.

Does Stonks track my portfolio or send price alerts?

No. Stonks doesn't track holdings, send price alerts, or convert prices into another currency. It shows prices and charts for the stocks you follow.

What does Stonks cost?

Nothing. Stonks is free and open source, under the MIT license.

INSTALL · NEEDS OMARCHY 4
$ omarchy plugin add https://github.com/gmchande/omarchy-stonks.git --enable
  1. Omarchy warns that plugins run unsandboxed and asks you to confirm.
  2. It asks where in the bar to put the pill. The center is the default.
  3. Click the pill.

To read the code first, leave out --enable, say no when Omarchy offers to enable it, and run omarchy plugin enable grvc.stonks when you're ready.
